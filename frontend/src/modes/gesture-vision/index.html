Gesture Vision Guide

Left hand

Fingers (scale degree)
1 → I   2 → II   3 → III   4 → IV   5 → V
Index + pinky → VI
Index + pinky + thumb → VII

Wrist tilt
Left/right of center → major/minor (there's a dead zone in the middle)

Right hand

Fingers (chord quality)
1 → root position   2 → 1st inversion   3 → 7th   4 → extended 7th

Thumb
Extended → octave down

Height
Higher → louder

Tilt
Left/right → sweeps the tone filter

Chord and quality changes are spoken aloud automatically — that's the one thing you can't infer from the sound alone. Volume and filter are conveyed through the sound itself.

Space bar

Press once to lock or unlock the voice (so accidental sounds don't interrupt you mid-performance).
Press twice to hear your current key and tone settings, then say a key or tone name to change it — press twice again to close without changing anything.
Press three times to hear this guide read aloud — press three times again to close it.
